Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Merlin Health is a single-user personal application operated by its owner for their own use.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app requests read-only access to the owner's own Google Health data: sleep, activity and fitness, and health metrics and measurements. It never writes to or modifies Google data.

Storage and use

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer, in an access-restricted local database, and is used solely for the owner's personal health analysis.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
